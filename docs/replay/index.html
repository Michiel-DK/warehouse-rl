    Every call, graded afterwards

      Top-20 hit rate: of the 20 most-flagged cells, how many sit on a hidden floor problem.

      The map is rules plus a decayed count of stalls per cell, and nothing is trained.

      Every call is graded afterwards, the count and rank grades against what happened next and the truth grade against the hidden layout, which the analyser never reads.

      Cells nobody drives over cannot be found, because a stall needs a robot to try to enter the cell.

      4. Lessons

      A lesson is one sentence the analyser can back with counts. Lessons come from fixed rules over stall and stuck counts (cell, robot, robot pair, hour, cause); there is no model and no learning. It is drawn when a time window of its zone is over, and graded when the next window of the same length is over (for an hour-of-day lesson, the same hours one day later): did it hold?

      This feed covers all zones of the shift, not only the zone drawn above; each line names its zone.

        Support is the low end of a 95 % Wilson interval on the count, divided by what the rule needs. It says how thin the counts are. It does not say how likely the lesson is to hold in the next window.
